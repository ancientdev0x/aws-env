# Bharat Builds Tour - Event 02: Environmental Hacks

Bad air, heatwaves, floods, water shortage and waste are problems we see every day. In this hackathon, you pick one of these problems and build something that helps solve it. It can be a problem in your city, your college or your home.

The hackathon runs online Thursday to Sunday, with an optional build day at DTU, Delhi, on Saturday, Oct 10. Limited seats, and you get workshops, project feedback, time with the Amazon team, and swag.

## Technical Eligibility & Ways to Build

To be eligible for prizes, your project needs to either use at least one AWS open source tool or be deployed on AWS. Beyond that, you are free to bring in any other tools you like.

### Two Ways to Build:

1. **Build It (Open source, on your machine)**
   * No AWS account, no card, no bill.
   * **Agents and AI**: Strands Agents SDK, PartyRock
   * **Containers and Kubernetes**: Finch, EKS Distro, EKS Anywhere
   * **Serverless**: SAM CLI, LocalStack
   * **Servers and runtimes**: Firecracker, Corretto
   * **Data and search**: OpenSearch
   * **Auth and policy**: Cedar

2. **Ship It (Deployed, with a URL)**
   * Free tier: up to $200 in credits to start (debit cards and RuPay work for signup, ~₹2 verification charge).
   * **Agents and AI**: SageMaker AI
   * **Containers and Kubernetes**: EKS, ECS, Fargate
   * **Serverless**: Lambda, API Gateway, Step Functions
   * **Servers and runtimes**: EC2, Lightsail, App Runner, Amplify Hosting
   * **Data and search**: S3, DynamoDB, RDS, Aurora
   * **Auth and policy**: Cognito
   * **Plumbing**: CloudFront, Route 53, EventBridge, SQS, SNS, CloudWatch

## Judging Criteria

1. **Idea and Impact**: Does it fix a real environmental problem? And what changes for the people living with it? A small problem solved well beats a big one solved vaguely.
2. **Built on AWS**: Build locally with AWS open-source tools, or deploy on AWS services. Using an AWS open-source project or AWS services is mandatory to win a prize.
3. **Design and usability**: Would someone who isn't on your team know what to do with it? Clear to pick up, easy to get around.
4. **The execution**: Does it work? Not perfect, not polished. Working. One feature that runs beats five that almost do.
5. **The demo video**: Three minutes, recorded, to show what it does, who it is for, and where AWS fits. No live demo; video is what judges see.
